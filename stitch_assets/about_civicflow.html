Neural Triage Architecture

How your voice travels from the screen to the street. Our pipeline integrates disparate modalities into a single, actionable flow.

account_tree Automated Routing Logic

The architecture leverages large-scale foundational models to extract semantic meaning from voice recordings, identify visual damage in images (pothole depth, leak severity), and categorize text descriptions. Once processed, tickets are contextually routed to BESCOM (Power), BWSSB (Water), or BBMP (Civic Works) via dedicated APIs, eliminating bureaucratic friction.
